How it behaves for playlists (all options)

Same engine as the ebook chapters, with three differences that matter for playlists.

Full text first, then speech.

Getting an article's full text is often what makes the next article wait, or fail with no signal. Preparing does both ahead, so the next article starts at once even in a tunnel.

Only text articles count.

Podcasts and YouTube already have Downloads. "Prepare ahead 2" means the next two text articles in the queue, however many podcasts sit between them.

Follows the queue, not the list.

Play next, reorder and remove change what's prepared. An Unread playlist that drops a read article keeps its queue, as today.

Played audio deletes itself.

When an article finishes, its audio goes. Skipped articles and anything not played within 7 days are cleared too.

Never stops reading.

If the next article isn't ready, the live voice reads it as today.

Also works from the feed list.

The same could prepare the next articles when read aloud continues down a feed (auto-continue). Not in these mockups; easy to add later.
